## How to read a contact document

Each has a contact brief, five chapter ranges, individually named objectives and small rewards,
upgrade requirements, relationship notes, sample dialogue, implementation limits, and source references.
Design labels such as QM11 are review references, not final persistent progression IDs.
Each contact has an explicitly authored introduction, two narrow branches, and a joined conclusion.
Introduction and conclusion lengths vary by contact; runtime sections show the actual structure.
Per-contact runtime sections list the actual branch nodes; equipment cross-links remain mandatory.

The November personality expansion adds a **Personality and personal conversation** section to every
profile, including the linked Old Timer document. It records motivations, voice boundaries, relationship
texture, and two new personal subjects with a contextual follow-up. These additions concern everyday life
in the Zone and are labelled as newly authored characterization. Existing history and progression
disclosures remain intact; the linked conversation files own the new in-game replies.

The remaining non-progression conversations have also been revised against these profiles, including
ordinary greetings, trade and service wording, background, advice, and existing relationship exchanges.
These edits preserve the existing conversation links and gameplay checks. Work and progression dialogue
are excluded from this pass.

Exactly half the steps in these new drafts use ordinary hand-ins or job checkpoints, alternating
hand-in and tracker across those slots. The other half are authored field or competitive objectives.
This is an initial authoring pattern, not a requirement to preserve rigid alternation in final play.

Trackers count only the contact's single repeatable category. C1, C2, etc. name cumulative checkpoints
within that contact's programme; they do not assign quantities. Preserve earned credit and define
when tracking starts before implementation. Arena counts eligible distinct sanctioned opponents.
Authored arena bouts need separately eligible results, not automatic completion from one prior win.

Hand-in pools, quantities, stock increments, prices, reward amounts, and generic equipment slots
remain unassigned where stated. Choose accessible existing items and publish the exact IDs in final
definitions. Unique story evidence is an authored field objective, not an ordinary hand-in.
Task equipment must not quietly grant the higher-tier item a progression is supposed to unlock.

Every claimed stock benefit requires a real catalogue item with an explicit cap. Different
ammunition calibres, optic roles, clothing colours, and protection trade-offs are not automatically
linear upgrades. Proven direct upgrades require their previous-tier milestone on the same character.

Late chapter names or a later map do not enforce access: implement explicit personal dependencies.
Never place a contact, receiving NPC, or claim terminal in Pripyat. Fixed-location field sites,
invented records and identities are proposals until mapped and implemented.

## Authored commission update

Half of the former 316 field commissions now use ordinary supply deliveries, split as evenly as possible
within each contact. The 158 remaining field commissions include 154 named bound-cargo assignments,
38 deliveries to another contact and three field-cache deliveries. Four retain research-defence cargo.
The per-contact story tables remain design references; converted steps supersede their earlier field
stories. See the [current commission and delivery inventory](../quest-commissions.md) for the exact
step IDs, requirement pools, quantities and surviving field assignments. Milestone IDs, prerequisites,
rewards and competitive bouts remain unchanged.

Finite deliveries now scale by storyline depth using FTS point counts and weighted availability.
See the [balance report](../delivery-balance.md) for the current pools, quantities and rarity estimates.
