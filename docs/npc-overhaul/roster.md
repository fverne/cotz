# Roster and progression arcs

[Design index](README.md). All rows below are proposals, not existing placement or finished quests.
The index's requirements and exclusions govern this roster. Existing personalities and models
must be checked against their current templates and dialogue before rewriting content.

Each row has exactly one repeatable category. Its difficulty changes targets and risk within
that category. Finite story objectives may use other activities. Chapter sequences below are
five-part outlines, with parallel specialist branches allowed within them.

See the [individual contact directory](contacts/README.md) for named steps, rewards, dialogue,
and prerequisite details. Those initial per-contact drafts use sequential chapters; optional
branching can be added during review while preserving equipment-tier gates.

## Category boundaries

- Industrial salvage: mechanical parts and workshop tools. Electronics: electronic components and devices.
  Utility supplies: operating consumables such as fuel and filters. Curate disjoint category item lists
  where these could overlap; Gasket must not become another Technut.
- Meal deliveries use cooked dishes; specimens use harvested parts; ammunition uses explicitly defined
  round/use units. Medical and textile supplies also need dedicated pools rather than generic valuables.
- Documents are ordinary discoverable records submitted through item delivery, not recovery cases.
- Equipment recovery is one search-and-return mechanic. No second recovery contact may appear on the same map.
- Sensor work includes placement, recording, and retrieval as one category. Research defence is a separate
  defended recording activity. Radio triangulation includes locating and collecting its source.
- Cleaner and Ledger both use dead-drop circuits, but live on different maps. Their cargo flavour differs;
  both retain the same category and completion contract.
- Spool now uses triangulation, replacing the earlier transmitter-deployment proposal.
- No town collectible-delivery category remains after removing the community NPC and Hoarder.
  Their old purchase unlocks still need coverage in the redistribution audit.

## Pripyat and access

No vendor, quest giver, reward terminal, or replacement NPC goes in Pripyat under this plan.
Players accept work and claim rewards elsewhere. Combine nearby objectives into viable expeditions.
Boss, Carbon, Wake, Atlas, the scientists, and other appropriate contacts can provide independent leads.
Ledger's Pripyat work uses unmanned caches, never a receiving town contact.

Keep ordinary survival and progression within each inhabited map viable. Waystation needs practical
food, medicine, and maintenance access through its catalogues or supply routes, without adding duplicate
job contacts. Basic availability is separate from advanced purchase unlocks. Exact travel gates are open;
do not make every optional contact or the competitive arena mandatory for Pripyat access.
